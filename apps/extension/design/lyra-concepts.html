machdown · three designs

All three use preset b3DpibPxNw: shadcn lyra style on Base UI, neutral palette, IBM Plex Sans for text, JetBrains Mono for headings. Lyra is already square-cornered and compact (32px buttons, 12px labels, 1px rings, no shadows), so the flat black look is the preset's own look with --background pushed to pure black.

Shared in every design: Save clip and Save all tabs are one click each. Every other action lives in a dropdown attached to the primary button. Search and Save-multiple open a full-window tab. The popup shows a rendered preview of the clip.
